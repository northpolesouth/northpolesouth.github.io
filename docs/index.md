# North Pole South

This year marks our 20th year of using Light-O-Rama to run our Christmas light show in Apache, Oklahoma. I remember the very first sequence I created from scratch: *Believe*, by Josh Groban. It took FOREVER! I spent every free minute for a week on it.

Technology has changed drastically since then. When we started, LED Christmas lights were a rare, pricey novelty, and smart pixels didn't exist. The only thing we knew about AI was the Will Smith movie *I, Robot*.

Twenty years later, it's a different landscape. Let me stress: I'm not a software engineer. I'm a Director of User Assistance Development at a major software company. In the olden days we called that technical writing. I've spent the past two years learning about AI and helping my team bring it into their daily work, so it seemed only natural to do the same to get the lights up and running this year. I was able to achieve a lot! These pages capture what I did, so anyone feeling adventurous can try it for themselves. (Fittingly, *Believe* was also the first song my new AI process lip-synced from start to finish.)

This is a personal hobby project, not connected to my employer.
